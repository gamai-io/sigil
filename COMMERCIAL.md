## Max pilot gate

Do not sell Max until all three are true:

1. The 1,000-task benchmark is public and reproducible.
2. The invoice -> ERP -> receipt demo runs on a clean machine.
3. At least 3 design partners have run one real workflow with evidence export.

## Why Max is not just tokens

- isolated worker, not a shared harness
- every action has a receipt and idempotency key
- audit export and replay
- human approval for irreversible effects
- support and onboarding

Prices are hypotheses. Test with 10 prospects before publishing a checkout.
